# Sunspell design and product challenge — requirements

Source: https://sunspell.fm/challenge/CHZwOBNI4_1WXSAljjYjj29yXKqht9z3RFh59GLatFo (printed 2026-09-23)
Deadline for this project: end of day 2026-09-24.

## Summary
Pick a product you wish existed, something you would genuinely open day to day,
and make a presentation introducing it. No theme, no category.

## Format
Build the presentation as a webpage (HTML file or React app) and hand it in as a
primary link that opens in the browser. Must be made with coding tools; AI is
encouraged. It needs to include three things:

1. **The idea, and the why.** A summary of what the product is and why it
   should exist, even if only for you.
2. **A clickable prototype flow.** The happy path, clickable end to end.
   Rough, wireframe-level screens are fine. Note edge cases or other flows
   you'd handle later; don't build them.
3. **One branded screen, with its moodboard.** One screen at full visual
   fidelity, plus the moodboard behind it, the references pulled from, and a
   short write-up of where the look came from and the decisions made.

## Judged on
The idea itself, how the presentation feels, storytelling, the flow, the
visual mock, and the taste behind it.

## Submitting
Up to five links. Deploy with Vercel Drop or Cloudflare Drop. Open and review
every link before submitting.

## FAQ highlights
- Rough flow is fine; save polish for the one branded screen.
- A sharp, niche idea beats a safe, broad one. It only needs to exist for you.
- No clock; they read the idea, the one screen, and the story.
